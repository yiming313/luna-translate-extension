使用方法How it works

三步，开始使用。Three steps to start.

填入 API KeyAdd your API key

在设置页选择服务商，粘贴 Key，点“测试连接”确认可用。Choose a provider in settings, paste your key and press “Test connection”.

打开弹窗Open the popup

点击工具栏里的 Luna 图标，输入或粘贴想处理的文字。Click the Luna icon in the toolbar, then type or paste your text.

翻译或润色Translate or polish

选择“翻译”或“润色”并点击按钮，也可以按 Ctrl/⌘ + Enter。结果马上出现在下方，可以一键复制。Pick Translate or Polish and press the button, or press Ctrl/⌘ + Enter. The result appears right below, ready to copy.

隐私Privacy

你的数据，只属于你。Your data stays yours.

没有 Luna 服务器，没有账号，没有统计。扩展只做一件事：把你输入的文字交给你自己选的 AI。No Luna server, no account, no analytics. The extension does one thing: hand the text you enter to the AI you chose.

Key 留在本机Your key stays on this device

API Key 保存在本机浏览器，不同步到你的 Google 账号。Your API key is kept in this browser and never synced to your Google account.

网页拿不到 KeyWeb pages never see it

只有扩展的后台脚本会读取 Key，你访问的网页无法读取。Only the extension's background worker reads the key — the pages you visit cannot.

直接发给你的服务商Straight to your provider

你输入的文字只发给你选的服务商，不经过任何第三方。The text you enter goes only to your chosen provider, never through a third party.

随时一键清除Delete everything, anytime

在设置页点一下，就能删除扩展保存的所有 Key 和设置。One click in settings removes every key and setting the extension stored.
